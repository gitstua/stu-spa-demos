A little about this page

Little Earners turns time and a chosen hourly reward into a live pocket-money counter. The timer starts automatically when the page opens; pause or begin again whenever you like. Your reward rate and timer are stored in the URL fragment, so you can bookmark or share this exact setup without an account. Money bags, beer, steak and whiskey rain down to cheer you on — most often in the first minute — tap or click any of them to pop it and score a point (your pop count for each treat is saved in the URL too), and the Celebrations button switches them off.
