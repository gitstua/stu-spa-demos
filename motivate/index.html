A little about this page

Little Earners turns time and a chosen hourly reward into a live pocket-money counter. Start, pause, or begin again whenever you like. Your reward rate, goal, and timer are stored in the URL fragment, so you can bookmark or share this exact setup without an account. Coins and beer rain down to cheer you on — most often in the first minute — and the Celebrations button switches them off.
