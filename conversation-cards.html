How it works

Cards are drawn without repeats until the deck is restarted or shuffled. Your deck, draw position, and optional Gist source are stored as a gzip-compressed, Base64URL-encoded query string in the URL fragment rather than browser storage. Open a Gist by ID with ?gist=GIST_ID, by URL with ?gist=https://gist.github.com/username/GIST_ID, or find one by name with ?gist=username/gist-name.
